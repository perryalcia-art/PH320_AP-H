About Me

I chose public and community health because I want to help communities of color facing disproportionate rates of chronic illness, maternal mortality, and barriers to healthcare access. One of the major health issues I care deeply about is the high rate of type 2 diabetes among people of color, and finding targeted ways to improve living conditions—such as access to healthy foods—in these communities. I am working toward becoming a Certified Community Health Educator leading a team in health promotion and outreach.
